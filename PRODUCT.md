# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

International retail buyers and distributors evaluating fresh produce suppliers. Producers are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose

Present FreshKampo's fresh-produce offering and commercial capabilities, and turn qualified international buyer interest into inquiries.

## Positioning

FreshKampo connects Mexican growing operations with retail and distribution markets. The confirmed primary markets are the United States, Europe, and the Middle East.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The existing site is a static, multi-page Spanish/English website with product, availability, producer, quality, certifications, company, and contact content.
- Preserve the current routes and language-switching behavior.
- Do not add business metrics or product/market claims unless they are supported by the project.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the brand name FreshKampo.
- The user wants a coherent, professional international-facing brand experience.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing project pages, content, and media in the repository.
- The public company site at https://freshkampo.com/.
- Metrics in the current project (including 230+ collaborators, 30+ retail clients, and 20+ years) are project-backed but should not be expanded or presented as new claims.

## Product Principles

- Make commercial information easy for international buyers to evaluate.
- Keep the bilingual experience and existing information architecture coherent.
- Prefer clear, substantiated product and quality information over unsupported claims.
- Make commercial contact the primary path to action.
